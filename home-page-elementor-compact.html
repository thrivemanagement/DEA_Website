Become a Certified Drug Educator.

Live online and private in-person training for people who work with young people.

Drug Educators Alliance offers live, instructor-led eight-hour training for educators, law enforcement, prevention professionals, youth leaders, parents and others who work with young people. Build practical communication skills, learn how to handle real situations and leave with ready-to-use curriculum and resources.

Live Online Training Choose a scheduled live training date and participate with an instructor through Zoom. Q4 Special: $199 per participant
Private In-Person Training Bring Drug Educators Alliance to your school, agency, organization or community for a private training.
