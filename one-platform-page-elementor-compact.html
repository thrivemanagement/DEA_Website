Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

ONE Platform is the planned digital foundation behind a future connected ecosystem of Drug Educators Alliance educational programs, courses, research, resources and measurement systems. It is currently in development.

The vision includes youth education, parent resources, educator and classroom tools, professional training and certification, First-Time Offender course infrastructure, workplace education, research and publications, and future measurement systems. Individual components are at different stages of development.

ONE Platform is in development. Drug Educators Alliance is working on planning, requirements, architecture, fundraising, content and technology partnerships. It should not yet be considered a fully launched platform.

Organizations interested in supporting the development of ONE Platform can review the Founding Partner opportunities or contact Drug Educators Alliance to discuss partnership possibilities.

Development is planned in phases. Drug Educators Alliance has not announced a final public launch date for the complete ONE Platform.
